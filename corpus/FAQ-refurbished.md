Harborline Certified Refurbished units are tested, graded, and sold with a 90-day warranty.

This 90-day term replaces the 12-month new-product warranty. It is the only warranty on refurbished Pulse Headphones, Tide Watch, and Beacon Speaker.

Refurbished Drift Laptops are not sold.

Harborline+ early access to refurbished drops does not extend this 90-day warranty.
