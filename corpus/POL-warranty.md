updated: 2026-03-01
tags: [policy, warranty]

New Harborline-branded electronics carry a 12-month limited warranty covering manufacturing defects.

The warranty does not cover accidental damage, liquid damage, or wear from normal use.

Warranty service is handled by Harborline Care. Customers should contact care@harborline.example with the serial number.

Third-party accessories sold on Harborline.com follow the manufacturer’s own warranty, not this policy.
