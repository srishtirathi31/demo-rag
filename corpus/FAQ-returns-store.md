updated: 2026-04-12
tags: [faq, returns]
---

Harborline stores accept returns of online orders if the customer shows the order email.

Store credit can be issued immediately. Card refunds still take 5–7 days after warehouse processing.

Not every mall kiosk can take Drift Laptop returns; customers should use a full-line store or ship back.
