Return window: electronics may be returned within 14 days of delivery. Items must be in original packaging with proof of purchase.

Harborline+ members may return electronics within 30 days of delivery.

Opened software, gift cards, and custom-engraved items cannot be returned.

Refunds go back to the original payment method within 5–7 business days after the warehouse receives the item.
