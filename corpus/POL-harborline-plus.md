updated: 2026-02-18
tags: [policy, membership]

Harborline+ costs $79 per year.

Members receive free express shipping, a 30-day electronics return window, and early access to refurbished drops.

Membership can be cancelled within 14 days of purchase for a full refund if no member-only benefit has been used.
