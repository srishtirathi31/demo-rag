Standard shipping is free on orders over $50 and arrives in 3–5 business days.

Express shipping is $12.99 and arrives in 1–2 business days.

Orders placed after 2pm Eastern ship the next business day.

Harborline does not ship to PO boxes for Drift Laptop orders.
