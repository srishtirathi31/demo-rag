Drift Laptop is a 14-inch notebook with a 16-hour battery and Harborline’s own keyboard.

It ships with a 65W USB-C charger. Using a lower-watt charger will slow charging and is unsupported.

Drift Laptop cannot be shipped to PO boxes.
