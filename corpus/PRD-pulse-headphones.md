Pulse Headphones are over-ear wireless headphones with 40 hours of battery life and active noise cancellation.

They ship with a USB-C cable and a 3.5mm analog cable. No wall charger is included.

Default warranty on new Pulse Headphones is 12 months.

Available colors: graphite, sand, and coastal blue.
